Prepared from local revision 72bc0c0 and the current uncommitted UI, 2 Nov 2026.

Everything below is fictional software-training data. No road was surveyed, no
PCI was validated, and no result will be approved or published. Passwords are
provided privately. This walkthrough uses the original PCI Automation project
if that is where the local app is connected. Saved branch, section, plan and
inspection records will remain there; signing out or clearing the preview does
not delete them. Record their labels for separately reviewed cleanup.

The local app currently enables the optional synthetic priority preview. If
"Open synthetic priority preview" is absent, the running build does not have
that demo setting enabled. The saved ranking/dashboard cannot be populated by
unapproved provisional inspections.
